Hamilton Depression Rating Scale (HAM-D / HDRS)

Score each item according to the patient's symptoms. Only the first 17 items count toward the total score.

Select the appropriate score for each item. Items 18–21 are recorded but are not included in the total score.
1. Depressed Mood – sadness, hopeless, helpless, worthless.
2. Feelings of Guilt – self-reproach, feels punished, delusions of guilt.
3. Suicide – wishes he/she were dead, thoughts of death, suicide attempts.
4. Insomnia (Early) – difficulty falling asleep.
5. Insomnia (Middle) – restless or disturbed during the night.
6. Insomnia (Late) – waking in early hours, unable to fall asleep again.
7. Work and Activities – loss of interest, decreased activity, hobbies dropped.
8. Psychomotor Retardation – slowed speech, decreased motor activity.
9. Agitation – restless, fidgety, unable to sit still.
10. Anxiety (Psychic) – worry, irritability, fears, panic attacks.
11. Anxiety (Somatic) – physical symptoms related to anxiety.
12. Somatic Symptoms (Gastrointestinal) – loss of appetite, constipation.
13. Somatic Symptoms (General) – heaviness in limbs, backache, loss of energy.
14. Genital Symptoms – loss of libido, menstrual disturbances.
15. Hypochondriasis – preoccupation with health.
16. Loss of Weight – patient-reported or measured.
17. Insight – acknowledges being depressed and ill.
18. Diurnal Variation (Not scored) – mood variation morning/evening.
19. Depersonalization/Derealization (Not scored) – feelings of unreality.
20. Paranoid Symptoms (Not scored) – suspiciousness, delusions of persecution.
21. Obsessive & Compulsive Symptoms (Not scored) – repetitive thoughts/behaviors.
