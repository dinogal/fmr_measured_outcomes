ASQ Suicide Risk Screening Tool

Screening Tool for Youth and Adults

Ask the patient

Complete Questions 1–4 first. If any answer to Questions 1–4 is Yes, then ask Question 5. A negative screen is No to all Questions 1–4. An acute positive screen is Yes to any of Questions 1–4 and Yes to Question 5. A non-acute positive screen is Yes to any of Questions 1–4 and No to Question 5.

1. In the past few weeks, have you wished you were dead?
2. In the past few weeks, have you felt that you or your family would be better off if you were dead?
3. In the past week, have you been having thoughts about killing yourself?
4. Have you ever tried to kill yourself?
5. Are you having thoughts of killing yourself right now?

Screening result and next steps

Result: Incomplete
Complete Questions 1–4 to generate a screening result.
Provide all patients with the crisis resources listed below.
Screening Result Reference
No to all Questions #1–#4

Negative screen

No intervention is necessary at this time.
Note: Clinical judgment can always override a negative screen.
Yes to any of Questions #1–#4 and Yes to Question #5

Acute positive screen

(imminent/acute risk identified)
  • Patient requires a STAT/urgent safety/full mental health evaluation.
  • Patient cannot leave until evaluated for safety.
  • Keep patient in sight.
  • Remove dangerous objects from room, if possible.
  • Alert clinician responsible for patient’s care.
Yes to any of Questions #1–#4 and No to Question #5

Non-acute positive screen

(potential risk identified)
  • Patient needs a brief suicide safety assessment to determine if a full mental health evaluation is needed and when.
  • Non-acute positive status does not require 1-to-1 observation while awaiting further assessment unless there are other safety concerns.
  • If adult patient, or parent/guardian of youth patient, refuses the brief suicide safety assessment, document the refusal.
  • Follow-up call is recommended.
  • Alert clinician responsible for patient’s care.
Exception note: when the positive screen is solely due to Yes on Question 4, a brief suicide safety assessment may not be necessary in certain situations based on age and how long ago the attempt occurred.

Provide resources to all patients

988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, and 988lifeline.org
Crisis Text Line: text HOME or HOLA to 741741, and crisistextline.org
